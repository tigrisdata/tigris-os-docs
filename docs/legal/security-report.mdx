# Report a Vulnerability

If you find a security issue in Tigris, email
[security@tigrisdata.com](mailto:security@tigrisdata.com). Include the affected
product or endpoint, steps to reproduce, and what you were able to access or
change. Do not include customer data in the report.

We acknowledge reports within a reasonable time and let you know when the issue
is fixed. With your agreement, we credit valid findings. We do not run a paid
bug bounty program.

:::note

The [Tigris Service Agreement](/docs/legal/service-terms/) does not permit
security or vulnerability testing of the service. If you notice an issue in the
course of normal use, report it as you found it. Do not probe further, and do
not read, modify, or delete data that is not yours.

:::

Our machine-readable contact details are published at
[tigrisdata.com/.well-known/security.txt](https://www.tigrisdata.com/.well-known/security.txt).
